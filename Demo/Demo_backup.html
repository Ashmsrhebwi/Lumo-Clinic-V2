Common Questions

Dental Implant
FAQ

Am I a candidate for dental implants?
+
Most adults with good general health qualify. A CT scan is used to assess bone density. Smokers or diabetics may require additional evaluation. We offer free pre-assessment reviews online.
How long does the procedure take?
+
Single implant placement: 45–90 minutes. Full-arch (All-on-4 or All-on-6): 3–5 hours per arch. Provisional teeth are fitted the same day. Final ceramic crowns are fitted 3–6 months later (available via our remote fitting partners).
Is the procedure painful?
+
No. The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia. You may experience mild pressure but no pain. Oral sedation and IV sedation are available for anxious patients at no extra cost.

Hollywood Smile:
The Process

A complete guide to the process, recovery, and results of Turkey's most sought-after dental makeover.

🦷
🦷

The Hollywood Smile has become synonymous with confidence, glamour, and personal reinvention. Every year, thousands of patients travel to Istanbul specifically for this treatment — but the information available online is often misleading, overly simplified, or frankly incorrect.

As the Aesthetic Dentistry Lead at Lumo Clinic, I've performed hundreds of smile transformations for patients from over 40 countries. In this guide, I want to share exactly what happens, what to expect, and how to make the best decision for your unique situation.

What Is a Hollywood Smile, Really?

A Hollywood Smile refers to a complete smile makeover using ultra-thin porcelain veneers. Unlike the thick, white, obviously-fake veneers of the 1990s, modern veneers — particularly E.Max lithium disilicate — are extraordinarily lifelike. They can be translucent, layered with colour variation, and shaped precisely to your facial structure.

The goal is not the whitest smile possible. The goal is the most beautiful smile for your face — and that's a crucial distinction.

The difference between a great smile and a Hollywood Smile is the difference between a good photograph and a portrait. Both are of you — only one is art.

The 5-Day Process at Lumo

Day 1 begins with Digital Smile Design — using facial photography, video analysis, and 3D scanning to design your smile before a single tooth is touched. You approve the design. We refine it until it's exactly right. This process alone often takes 2–3 hours and is one of the most important steps.

Days 2–3 involve tooth preparation (minimal, under anaesthesia), impressions, and temporary veneers. Days 4–5 are the bonding sessions where your final custom veneers are fitted, adjusted, and permanently bonded. You leave Istanbul with your complete new smile.

The Questions You Should Ask Any Clinic

Before booking, always ask: What veneer material do you use? (E.Max is the gold standard.) Who designs the smile? (A specialist — not a technician.) Can I see unprompted before-and-after photographs from real patients? Do you have a Digital Smile Design preview? What is included in the price?

At Lumo, the answers to all of these are transparent and verifiable. Our all-inclusive package covers consultation, DSD design, temporary veneers, permanent veneers, all appointments, medications, and airport transfer. No hidden fees — ever.

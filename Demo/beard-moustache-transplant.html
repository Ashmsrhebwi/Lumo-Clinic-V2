Treatment FAQ

Your Questions
Answered

Where does the donor hair come from?
+
For beard transplants, the hair is usually taken from the back or sides of the scalp, similar to a regular hair transplant. If needed, we can also use hair from the bottom of the chin (neck area).
Can I shave my transplanted beard?
+
Yes. After the initial healing period (about 2 weeks), you can shave or trim your new beard exactly like natural facial hair. It will continue to grow back normally.
How many grafts do I need for a full beard?
+
A typical beard transplant ranges from 1,500 to 3,500 grafts depending on the desired density and whether you are filling in patches or creating a full beard.

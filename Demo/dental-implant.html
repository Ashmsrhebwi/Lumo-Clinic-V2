Treatment FAQ

Your Questions
Answered

Is the procedure painful?
+
With modern local anesthesia and sedation options, the procedure is virtually painless. Most patients report only mild discomfort for 24-48 hours after the surgery, easily managed with standard pain relief.
How long is the recovery period?
+
The initial healing takes about 7-10 days. The "osseointegration" process, where the implant fuses with the bone, takes 3-6 months. We provide temporary crowns during this period so you never leave without a smile.
How long do dental implants last?
+
With proper oral hygiene and regular check-ups, dental implants are designed to be a lifetime solution. The titanium root fuses permanently with your jawbone.

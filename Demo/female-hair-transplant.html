Bespoke Female
Hair Restoration

Specialized "No-Shave" DHI techniques designed for the unique patterns of female hair thinning. Natural density and elegance without compromise.

Elegance and Confidence

Female hair restoration requires a significantly different approach than male procedures. We focus on diffuse thinning and hairline softening, using advanced DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) to place grafts between existing hairs without the need for shaving.

Our surgeons understand the emotional impact of hair loss for women and provide a discreet, supportive, and world-class medical environment.

Treatment FAQ

Your Questions
Answered

Will anyone know I had the procedure?
+
With our no-shave DHI technique, the procedure is extremely discreet. Most patients can return to work and social activities within 2-3 days without anyone noticing the surgery.
What causes female hair loss?
+
Hair loss in women can be caused by genetics, hormonal changes (menopause), stress, or medical conditions. We perform a full diagnostic check to ensure you are a good candidate for transplant.
When will I see the full results?
+
Hair growth is a gradual process. You will see initial growth around 4 months, with 70-80% of the result visible at 9 months, and final density achieved at 12-14 months.

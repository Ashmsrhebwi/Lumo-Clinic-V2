Treatment FAQ

Your Questions
Answered

How many days do I need in Istanbul?
+
A typical Hollywood Smile makeover requires 5-7 days. This allows for the initial preparation, the laboratory crafting of your veneers, and the final fitting and adjustments.
What is the difference between E-Max and Zirconium?
+
E-Max is known for its superior translucency and is ideal for front teeth (veneers). Zirconium is exceptionally strong and is often used for full crowns or when extra durability is needed. Our doctors will recommend the best material for your specific case.
Will my teeth look "fake"?
+
Not at Lumo. We avoid the "piano key" look by customizing the shade, translucency, and micro-texture of each veneer to mimic the natural variation of real teeth.

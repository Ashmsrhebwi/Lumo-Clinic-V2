Ask a Question →

● 24/7 Priority Patient Support

How do I get started from abroad?
+
Begin with a free online consultation via WhatsApp or our form. Share photos if relevant, and our specialist team will provide a personalised treatment plan and quote within 24 hours. We handle all logistics from there.
How long should I plan to stay in Istanbul?
+
Most treatments require 3–7 days. Hollywood Smile typically takes 5 days. Hair transplants need 2–3 days in clinic. We provide a full pre-trip timeline and help arrange accommodation nearby.
Are your prices all-inclusive?
+
Yes. Our packages include the procedure, medications, aftercare kit, follow-up consultations, and airport transfers. Accommodation packages are available upon request. Zero hidden fees — always.
What makes Lumo different from other Istanbul clinics?
+
We're not a volume clinic. We limit daily patient intake to ensure surgeon focus, premium materials, and personalised aftercare. Our NPS score of 94 speaks for itself.
Do you provide aftercare once I return home?
+
Absolutely. Your dedicated coordinator is available via WhatsApp for the full recovery period. We schedule video check-ins and provide a detailed home-care protocol tailored to your procedure.

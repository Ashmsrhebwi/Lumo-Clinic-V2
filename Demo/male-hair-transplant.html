Treatment FAQ

Your Questions
Answered

Will the result look natural?
+
Absolutely. The key to a natural hair transplant is the design of the hairline and the angle of graft placement. Our surgeons are artists who ensure your new hair blends seamlessly with your existing features.
How many grafts will I need?
+
This depends on your level of hair loss (Norwood scale) and your donor area density. We provide a personalized graft estimate during your free online consultation.
Is the hair transplant permanent?
+
Yes. The hair follicles taken from the back of the scalp are genetically resistant to hair loss and will continue to grow for a lifetime in their new position.

Treatment FAQ

Your Questions
Answered

How long do I need to stay in Istanbul?
+
For major surgeries like rhinoplasty or body contouring, we recommend a stay of 7-10 days. This allows for the procedure, initial recovery, and the critical follow-up check before you fly home.
Is general anesthesia safe?
+
Yes. All our surgeries are performed in JCI-accredited hospitals by senior anesthesiologists. We perform thorough pre-op tests (blood work, EKG, etc.) to ensure you are fully fit for surgery.
Will there be visible scars?
+
Our surgeons use "hidden incision" techniques whenever possible. For example, in closed rhinoplasty, all incisions are inside the nose. For body procedures, we place incisions in natural skin folds where they are virtually undetectable after healing.
